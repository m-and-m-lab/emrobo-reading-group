One robot, many representations

By embodiment we mean the physical properties and constraints that shape how a robot can act: morphology, kinematics, dynamics, contacts, sensing, actuation limits, controller interfaces, latency and stability. We want to know which of these a method actually represents, how it uses them, and what that buys.
