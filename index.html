Reading group · University of Michigan

Embodiment Reading Group

We explore how representations of a robot's body and physical constraints unlock capabilities for task performance, planning and control. We read work where morphology, kinematics, dynamics, contact and sensing are modeled as first-class structure, with a focus on mobile and dexterous manipulation.

Monthly · 1–2 hours

Tooling Hour

Once a month, someone walks the group through a tool others can actually use: an open-source codebase, a simulator, a hardware setup or a workflow. The useful part is what rarely makes it into papers: tricks, limitations and setup issues.

Practical and informal. A terminal and a simulator window are welcome; slides are optional.
