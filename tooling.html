How it works

The presenter shares an open-source tool, codebase, simulator, hardware setup or workflow, shown working, and the practical lessons that came with it. It's meant to stay practical and informal rather than become another presentation: a terminal and a simulator window are welcome, and several short demos can share one hour.

Once a month, on the Tuesday between two reading sessions: same time, same room, same Zoom link. It doesn't replace a paper session.

First session: Spot, from USD asset to policy

A walkthrough of the Spot USD asset and an Isaac Sim / Isaac Lab training pipeline for mobile manipulation: where the robot's structure, physical properties and control interface live, and how they reach the policy.

USD asset → articulation (joints, limits, actuators) → task (observations, actions, rewards) → RL in parallel environments → policy on hardware.
