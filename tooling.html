How it works

  • Something people can use. An open-source tool, codebase, simulator, hardware setup or workflow, shown working.
  • The practical lessons. Tricks, limitations and setup issues: the parts that rarely make it into a paper or a README.
  • Practical and informal. It shouldn't become another presentation session. A terminal and a simulator window are welcome, and several short demos can share one hour.
  • Once a month, 1–2 hours. Same room and Zoom link as the reading group; recorded only with the presenter's consent.

Topic ideas

Anything that makes embodiment research easier to do is fair game.

  • URDF and USD robot models
  • Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab workflows
  • Extracting kinematic and dynamic information from robot models
  • Building graphs or tokens from robot structure
  • Robot asset modification
  • Morphology and dynamics randomization
  • Force, torque, contact and tactile sensing
  • Mobile manipulation training pipelines
  • Skill-learning pipelines
  • Hardware data collection
  • Sim-to-real workflows
  • Cross-embodiment evaluation
  • Visualization and debugging tools
  • Useful open-source repositories
