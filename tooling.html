What these are

Tooling sessions run 1–2 hours and are meant to be practical and informal. Share tools you actively use, lessons from implementation, work in progress, or the limitations you've hit. Polished slides are optional; a terminal and a simulator window are welcome.

The first planned session walks through the Spot USD asset and an Isaac Sim training pipeline, including how the robot's structure, physical properties and control interfaces are represented.

Possible topics

  • URDF and USD robot models
  • Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab workflows
  • Extracting kinematic and dynamic information
  • Building graphs and tokens from robot models
  • Robot asset modification
  • Morphology and dynamics randomization
  • Force, torque, contact and tactile sensing
  • Hardware data collection
  • Policy training across embodiments
  • Sim-to-real workflows
  • Visualization and debugging tools
  • Useful repositories and packages
